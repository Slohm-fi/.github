# Slohm Finance

**Treasury-backed staking and bonding on Bitcoin.**

Slohm Finance brings decentralized reserve currency mechanics to Bitcoin Layer 1 via [OPNet](https://opnet.org). Stake, bond, and earn — all secured by Bitcoin's base layer.

---

## What is Slohm?

Slohm is a protocol that lets you grow your Bitcoin-native holdings through two core mechanisms:

### Staking
Deposit SLOHM tokens and receive ySLOHM — a rebasing token whose balance automatically increases over time. No need to claim or compound. Just hold ySLOHM and watch your balance grow with every rebase epoch.

### Bonding
Purchase SLOHM at a discount by providing reserve assets (like MOTO). Bonds vest over a set period, and you receive a transferable Bond Note NFT representing your position. Claim your vested SLOHM as it unlocks — partially or all at once.

### Treasury
Every SLOHM token is backed by real reserve assets held in the protocol's treasury. The treasury enforces a minimum backing ratio, ensuring intrinsic value for all token holders.

---

## How It Works

1. **Stake** your SLOHM to receive ySLOHM
2. **Earn** automatic rebase rewards each epoch — your ySLOHM balance grows
3. **Unstake** when you're ready — a short cooldown period applies before withdrawal
4. **Bond** reserve assets for discounted SLOHM if you want to grow your position faster
5. **Claim** vested bonds as they unlock over the vesting period

---

## Key Features

- **Automatic Rebasing** — ySLOHM balances increase every epoch with no user action required
- **Transferable Bond Notes** — Bonds are issued as NFTs that can be traded on secondary markets
- **Treasury-Backed** — Every SLOHM is backed by protocol-held reserves
- **Bitcoin Native** — Built on OPNet, secured by Bitcoin L1 consensus
- **No Intermediaries** — Fully on-chain smart contracts, no centralized components

---

## Protocol Components

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **SLOHM** | The protocol's native token, minted against treasury reserves |
| **ySLOHM** | Rebasing staked token — hold it to earn rewards automatically |
| **Treasury** | Holds reserve assets and enforces backing guarantees |
| **Staking** | Manages stake/unstake flows and triggers rebases |
| **Bond Depository** | Issues bonds at market-driven prices with configurable terms |
| **Bond Notes** | NFT receipts for bond positions (transferable, partially redeemable) |
| **Distributor** | Calculates and distributes rebase rewards each epoch |
